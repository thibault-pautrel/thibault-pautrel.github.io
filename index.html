Welcome to my personal page

I am currently an intern at the LISTIC laboratory in Annecy, under the supervision of Guillaume Ginolhac (LISTIC), Ammar Mian (LISTIC), and Florent Bouchard (Centrale-Supélec). My research focuses on Riemannian federated learning for time series analysis applications using remote sensing data.

This internship is part of ENSAI's Master for Smart Data Science, in which I have been enrolled since September 2024.

Previously, I was a Ph.D. student at Université de Rennes 1 (IRMAR), under the supervision of Jürgen Angst and Guillaume Poly.
