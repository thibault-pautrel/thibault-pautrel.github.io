Teaching

Current teaching (2025–2026)

Introduction to programming with C++

Institution: IUT d’Orsay – computer lab sessions (33 h).

Overview: Introductory course in algorithmic thinking and structured programming using modern C++.

Topics include:

  • Basic C++ syntax, types, input/output.
  • Control structures (conditionals, loops).
  • Functions and modular program design.
  • Fundamental data structures (arrays, vectors, structs).
  • Introduction to object-oriented programming (classes, encapsulation).
  • Implementation of classical searching and sorting algorithms.

Machine learning tutorials

Institution: CentraleSupélec – second year (2A), tutorials (12 h).

Overview: Practical sessions accompanying a core introduction to machine learning.

Topics include:

  • Linear and logistic regression, model evaluation.
  • Supervised classification and support vector machines.
  • Dimensionality reduction (e.g. PCA).
  • Unsupervised learning (clustering methods).
  • Ensemble methods (bagging, boosting, random forests).
  • Short introduction to deep learning concepts.

Previous teaching experience

Between September 2021 and July 2024, I was assigned as an “agrégé” mathematics teacher to different secondary schools: Collège Saint-Exupéry (Villiers-le-Bel), Lycée Blaise Pascal (Châteauroux), and Lycée Bernard Palissy (Gien). This diversity of student levels further strengthened my pedagogical skills and classroom experience.

During my PhD (September 2018 – September 2021), I taught 64 hours per year for the mathematics department of the University of Rennes :

  • Analysis and Probability (L2 mathematics): Taylor expansions, Riemann integrals, and introductory probability theory.
  • Probability (L1 biology): general probability theory, Bayes’ formula, Central Limit Theorem, and Law of Large Numbers.

Supervision

  • Supervision of study-and-research dissertations on Stein’s method for undergraduate ENS Rennes students.
  • Preparation for the “agrégation” oral examination (Probability and Statistics option).

Assessment

Examiner for the Mathematics and Computer Science oral examinations (2023, 2024) and marker for the written MP examinations (2023, 2024, 2025) for CentraleSupélec.
