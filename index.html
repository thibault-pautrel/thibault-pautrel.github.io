Welcome to my personal page

Since October 2025, I have been a postdoctoral researcher at the L2S research laboratory https://l2s.centralesupelec.fr.

My work focuses on federated learning on covariance matrices with SPDNet for Riemannian models on health data (EEG).

Previously, I was enrolled in the Master “Statistics for Smart Data” at ENSAI (Rennes) to strengthen my background in machine learning and computer science. Thanks to this program, I was able to transition from my previous position as a mathematics teacher in secondary education and come back to the academic research world I had left after my PhD thesis. In 2022, I was granted a PhD from the University of Rennes for my work on the universality of the zeros of random functions. My PhD was supervised by Guillaume Poly and Jürgen Angst, and the manuscript can be found here.
