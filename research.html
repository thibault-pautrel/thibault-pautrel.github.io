Research

My research lies at the interface of Riemannian geometry, statistical learning, and signal processing. It is organised around three connected themes.

  • Geometry-aware federated learning Federated training of SPDNet on covariance-based representations of EEG signals, with Riemannian aggregation schemes and privacy guarantees. Current, since 2025.
  • Riemannian stochastic optimization Stochastic optimization on Riemannian manifolds, motivated by sufficient dimension reduction, where the targeted subspace is a point on the Grassmann manifold. Ongoing.
  • Zeros of random trigonometric functions Universality of the expected number of real zeros under dependent and non-Gaussian coefficients. 2019–2022.
